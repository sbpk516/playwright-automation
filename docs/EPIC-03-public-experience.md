# EPIC-03: Public Experience

## Goal

Give visitors an accessible public introduction to StreamForge and its fictional subscription plans.

## Scope

- Responsive landing page with service explanation.
- Sign-in and plan comparison actions.
- Display of three seeded plans with price, quality, stream limit, and feature differences.
- Clear fictional/demo pricing labels.
- Redirection of subscriber-only actions to sign-in with a safe return path.

## Requirement Coverage

- LAND-001 through LAND-004
- PLAN-001 display requirement
- Visitor portions of User Experience and Accessibility Requirements

## Acceptance

- Visitors can understand the service, compare all seeded plans, and reach sign-in using keyboard or pointer input.
- Pricing and plan behavior are unambiguously labeled as simulated.
- Subscriber-only actions preserve the intended destination through authentication.
- The page works at documented mobile, tablet, and desktop reference widths.

## Exclusions

- Selecting or changing a subscriber plan.
- Search-engine optimization beyond correct page metadata and semantic HTML.
